# DoodhDirect PRD

Use the PRD already provided in the project conversation as the product authority. Core scope:

- NIT Faridabad pilot, 3–4 buffaloes, 15–20 families.
- Loose fresh buffalo milk initially; future product model supports khoya, ghee, paneer, curd and more.
- One-time orders and prepaid subscriptions.
- Customer-selected delivery days for subscriptions; no time-slot selection.
- Skip/pause without consuming prepaid entitlement for undelivered dates.
- Customer addresses with map coordinates.
- Nearest-branch allocation from day one; future multi-branch support mandatory.
- Delivery staff use same role-aware Flutter app.
- OTP delivery confirmation.
- Active-delivery GPS tracking.
- Free doorstep milk testing on request using current lactometer/physical-device model.
- Configurable 5-hour complaint window.
- Photo/video complaint attachments.
- Support/admin approval for replacement or refund.
- Repeated complaint alerts.
- Selected 24x7 dairy live cameras for authenticated customers.
- Dairy production, daily milk batches and testing records.
- Razorpay, UPI/card/netbanking/COD/wallet support.
- Coupons/referrals.
- Owner has global access; other roles use RBAC and optional branch scope.
- Reports/export to CSV/XLSX.
- India-specific compliance readiness including FSSAI, privacy and future GST/invoicing.

For complete screen/module/business-rule detail, use the other documents in this package.
